Use it well.

EFWH should make the model harder to fool, not make the process heavier.

1
Describe the outcome, not your favorite solution.

Let orientation and evidence decide the path. Add hard constraints only when they are real.

2
Connect the sources that matter.

Internal docs, code, lineage, logs, warehouse access, test rigs, and SMEs can matter more than model IQ.

3
Buy autonomy with containment.

A disposable worktree or sandbox is better than twenty meaningless approval clicks.

4
Make “impossible” earn its name.

A failed narrow experiment rules out that path—not the entire technology or hypothesis space.

What appears in the workspace?
