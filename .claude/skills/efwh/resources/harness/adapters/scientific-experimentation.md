# Adapter — Scientific / Engineering Experimentation

## Orientation additions

Identify existing datasets, instruments/simulators, calibration information, prior experiments, code/notebooks, literature, and reproducibility constraints.

## Experiment discipline

Record hypothesis, controls, variables, environment/configuration, random seeds where applicable, data versions, and predicted observations before the run.

Avoid HARKing: do not rewrite the pre-test hypothesis as though an unexpected post-hoc pattern was predicted.

## Evaluation

Use domain-appropriate statistics, uncertainty estimates, replication, ablations/controls, and SME review. A model-generated narrative is never a substitute for the measured result.
